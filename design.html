Design Services

At Dogwood Homes, our design team collaborates closely with you to transform your vision into a home that reflects your lifestyle and aesthetic. Whether you're reimagining a kitchen, updating a bathroom, selecting a cohesive color palette, or furnishing an entire residence, we provide comprehensive design services tailored to your needs. Our experienced designers guide you through every decision, ensuring a seamless and enjoyable process.

Utilizing our integrated project management system, we offer real-time updates, design boards, and transparent budgeting to keep you informed and involved. Serving homeowners and vacation rental owners in Little Rock and Central Arkansas, Dogwood Homes is dedicated to delivering spaces that are not only beautiful but also practical and inviting.

Dogwood Homes Design Services

Initial Consultation

Embarking on your project begins with a one-hour consultation—a relaxed meeting to discuss your ideas and aspirations. During this session, we'll explore broad concepts and brainstorm possibilities. Following our discussion, you'll receive a summary outlining the project's scope, an estimated timeline, and a statement of work.

  • $100 Virtual / $150 In-Person
  • Duration: 1 Hour
  • Does not include a formal cost estimate
Virtual Room Design Package

Ideal for clients managing projects remotely or working within a budget, this service empowers you to implement a professionally curated design at your own pace. Our team will develop a comprehensive design plan tailored to your space, including selections for wall and window treatments, furniture, linens, and rugs.

  • $1,000 per Room
  • Timeline: Approximately 3 Months
  • A detailed spreadsheet with direct links to all selected items and an estimated project cost
  • One round of design revisions to ensure alignment with your vision
  • A one-hour virtual presentation to walk you through the design concept
Comprehensive Room Design

Our Comprehensive Room Design service offers a complete, start-to-finish interior design experience. Ideal for clients seeking a fully managed project, this service ensures every detail is thoughtfully considered and expertly executed.

  • Starting at $3,000 per Room (Design Fee)
  • Timeline - Approximately 3+ Months
  • Personalized Consultation - We begin by understanding your lifestyle, design preferences, and vision to tailor the space to your needs.
  • Customized Design Plan - Receive a detailed design board with one round of revisions to refine the concept to your satisfaction.
  • Product Procurement - We handle the sourcing and ordering of all furnishings and materials, providing access to exclusive trade discounts.
  • Project Coordination - Our team manages communication and coordination with contractors and subcontractors to ensure seamless execution.
  • Installation Oversight - We oversee the delivery and installation of all design elements, bringing the finalized vision to life.
  • This all-inclusive service is designed to transform your space into a cohesive and functional environment that reflects your personal style.
Full-Service Home Design & Installation

Experience a comprehensive design journey from concept to completion. Ideal for new builds or extensive remodels, this service encompasses:

  • Personalized Consultation: Understanding your lifestyle and design preferences.
  • Complete Finish & Fixture Selection: From flooring and cabinetry to bathroom fixtures and garage doors.
  • Furnishings & Décor Curation: Including furniture, linens, artwork, wall coverings, and accessories.
  • Detailed Design Boards: Customized for each room with revisions as needed.
  • Collaborative Coordination: Working alongside architects, builders, and subcontractors.
  • Product Procurement: Access to trade discounts and management of all orders.
  • Progress Meetings: At least three sessions (virtual or in-person) to review selections and timelines.
  • This turnkey service ensures a cohesive and stress-free transformation of your home, tailored to your unique vision.
  • Starting at $20,000 (Design Fee)
  • Timeline: 8+ Months
Vacation Rental Full Home Design

Transform your vacation rental into a standout destination with our comprehensive design service. Ideal for property owners aiming to enhance guest appeal and maximize bookings, this package offers:

The Design Process

At Dogwood Homes, we believe that thoughtful design is the cornerstone of every successful project. Our structured approach ensures a seamless journey from initial concept to final realization. We begin by understanding your lifestyle, preferences, and vision for your space. Our design team creates tailored concepts, incorporating your ideas and functional needs. We refine the selected concept, finalizing layouts, materials, and finishes to align with your vision.

Detailed plans and schedules are developed to guide the renovation process smoothly. Our team oversees the implementation, ensuring quality and attention to detail throughout the construction phase. We conclude with a comprehensive review to ensure every aspect meets your expectations. By following this structured approach, we aim to transform your space into an environment that is both beautiful and functional, reflecting your unique style and needs.
